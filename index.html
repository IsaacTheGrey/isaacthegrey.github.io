01 / Research

From molecules to biological rhythms

How does life
keep time?

☼◐

Sunlight. Moonlight. Molecular clocks.

I study the molecular mechanisms that connect the rhythms of the sun and moon to the rhythms of living organisms.

As a PhD candidate at the University of Vienna, I investigate how moonlight and the circalunar clock shape circadian plasticity in the marine bristleworm Platynereis dumerilii.

My work combines experimental molecular biology, transcriptomics, behavioural tracking, and computational models of coupled oscillators. I work in the Department of Neuroscience and Developmental Biology, supervised by Prof. Kristin Tessmar-Raible.
